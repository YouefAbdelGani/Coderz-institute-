article[data-status="full"] .status

This selector matches the <span class="status"> element that is inside
an <article> whose data-status attribute equals "full". In my page, that
is the status text of the CSS Foundations card. It does not match the
<article> itself, the <h2>, or the <p> description, because the space
(descendant combinator) moves the match from the article down to the
span with class "status" inside it. That is why only the status label
turns gray and the description stays on the normal card background.
